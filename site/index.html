The Concept

Two rituals, one house.

The Back Rooms started with a simple idea — that a great haircut and a genuine moment of stillness shouldn't require two different trips across the city. So we built both, side by side.

The Barbershop

Old-world barbering with a modern hand — clean fades, straight-razor shaves, and beard work done with real attention, in chairs built for a long, unhurried sit.

  • Precision cuts & skin fades
  • Hot towel straight-razor shaves
  • Beard sculpting & grey blending

The Therapy Lounge

Our signature back scratch rituals — a deliberately old-fashioned, deeply calming service performed in a dim, warm room built purely for unwinding.

  • Signature & deep-pressure back scratch
  • Combined back & scalp rituals
  • Private couples sessions
