Two rooms, one house.

The Back Rooms started with a simple idea: a great haircut and a genuine moment of stillness shouldn't take two trips across the city. So we built both, side by side, and kept the lights low.

The Barbershop

Old-world barbering with a modern hand. Clean fades, straight-razor shaves and beard work done with real attention, in chairs built for a long, unhurried sit.

  • Precision cuts and skin fades
  • Hot towel straight-razor shaves
  • Beard sculpting and grey blending

The Scratch Lounge

Our signature back and scalp scratch sessions. A deliberately old-fashioned, deeply calming hour of attention in a dim, warm room built for nothing but unwinding.

  • Quick, Standard and Extended sessions
  • Back and scalp attention, every visit
  • Monthly Lounge memberships

Good to know

A few house customs, and answers to what new guests usually ask.

What happens in a Scratch Lounge session?

You settle into a reclined lounge chair in a dim, quiet room, fully clothed. Your host spends the session on light, slow scratching across your back and scalp, adjusting pressure to whatever you prefer. There's no oil and nothing to change into. Just tell us what feels good.

Is this massage or therapy?

No. Our Lounge hosts are not licensed massage therapists, and sessions are not medical or therapeutic treatment. Think of it as a calm, old-fashioned moment of attention. If you have a skin condition or recent injury, let us know when you book.

Do you take walk-ins?

We work by appointment so nobody waits. If a chair or the Lounge is open, we're happy to fit you in, but booking ahead is the only way to guarantee a time.

What if I'm running late or need to cancel?

Please give us at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or move an appointment. If you're more than 10 minutes late, we may need to shorten your session so the next guest isn't kept waiting.

How do memberships work?

Memberships renew monthly through Square, and your sessions reset at the start of each billing month. Tier 3 members get priority booking and can split one 45-minute session into two shorter visits.

Can I book a cut and a session together?

Yes, and many guests do. Book your barber and your Lounge session back to back and we'll keep the handover between rooms seamless.

House customs
  • Phones on silent in the Scratch Lounge.
  • Keep voices low past the front room.
  • Arrive five minutes early to settle in.
  • Gratuity for barbers and hosts is appreciated, never expected.
